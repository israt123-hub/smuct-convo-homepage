FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact your Registrar's Office.

Who is eligible to register?

Graduates who have completed all required credits for Bachelor’s or Master’s programs by the stipulated deadline and received official approval from the controller of examinations are eligible to register.

What if my name isn't on the eligible list?

If your name or ID is not appearing on the eligible list, please get in touch with the Registrar’s Office or the academic department with your provisional certificate or transcript copy to verify your eligibility status.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept mobile financial services (bKash, Nagad, Rocket), online banking portals, and bank slip uploads depending on the specified payment options inside the portal.

How do I get my entry pass?

Once your submitted documents and payment verification are approved, the Gate Pass will be available for download under your portal dashboard.

Can I bring guests?

Yes, each graduate can register up to a maximum of 2 guests during the online registration process.
